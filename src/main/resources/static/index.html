Connect your browser

Four steps, about two minutes, done once. After this, job scores appear on Naukri and Indeed.

  1. 1

    Download the extension

    A small add-on for Chrome, Brave or Edge.

    Download extension

    Unzip it somewhere permanent, such as Documents. The browser keeps reading it from that folder, so do not delete it afterwards.

  2. 2

    Add it to your browser

    Open a new tab and go to chrome://extensions (browsers do not allow a clickable link to this page).

    1. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
    2. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped jobpilot-extension folder.
    3. Optional: pin the JobPilot icon from the puzzle-piece menu.

    Then come back and reload this page.

  3. 3

    Connect

    Looking for the extension…

  4. 4

    Use it on job sites

    Open Naukri Open Indeed

    Sign in as usual. On a search page every job gets a JobPilot score badge. Open a job and a JobPilot panel appears at the bottom right with the score, the skills you have and lack, and Tailor resume & queue.

    Also fills forms on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, Workable, SmartRecruiters, iCIMS and Indeed smart apply. Already had a Naukri tab open? Reload it once.

    No score showing? The panel on the job site tells you why (for example "Not connected" or "JobPilot is not running").
